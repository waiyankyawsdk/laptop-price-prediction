RESEARCH METHOD

How the evaluation works

1. Target

The CSV Price column is treated as Indian rupees. Training uses the natural logarithm of price to reduce the influence of extreme premium laptops; all user-facing errors and predictions are converted back to INR.

2. Split

{{ metadata.dataset_rows }} rows are divided into {{ metadata.split.train }} training, {{ metadata.split.calibration }} calibration, and {{ metadata.split.test }} untouched testing records using random state {{ metadata.random_state }}.

3. Model selection

Ridge, Gradient Boosting, Random Forest, and Extra Trees use identical five-fold training partitions. The lowest mean cross-validation log-RMSE selects the final model: {{ metadata.selected_model }}.

5. Limitation

This is a baseline reproduction using an old, small static dataset. It does not prove present-day US or Indian retail accuracy. A production study needs dated multi-retailer data, exact CPU/GPU generations, region, discounts, and time-based evaluation.
